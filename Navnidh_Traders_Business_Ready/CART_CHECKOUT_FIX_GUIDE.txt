NAVNIDH TRADERS - STOCK, CART AND BUY NOW FIX

1. Extract this ZIP to D:\Navnidh_Traders_Business_Ready (back up the old folder first).
2. Open CMD and run:
   cd /d D:\Navnidh_Traders_Business_Ready
   setup.bat
3. Set real stock and selling prices (required before purchase buttons enable):
   setup_inventory.bat
   Enter the physically verified stock count and final price for each product. Do not invent stock.
4. Start both apps:
   run_both.bat
5. Open http://127.0.0.1:5000 and hard-refresh with Ctrl+F5.
6. Product cards and product pages show exact database stock quantity. Add to cart and Buy now submit to the cart_add route; Buy now redirects to checkout after adding.
7. If stock is 0, purchase buttons remain disabled intentionally. This prevents orders for unavailable products.
8. Test COD checkout with test details. Online payment requires valid Razorpay merchant keys and a verified test payment.

IMPORTANT: This package cannot know your real shop inventory. Stock must be entered by the owner.
