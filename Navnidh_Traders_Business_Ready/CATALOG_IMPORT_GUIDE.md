# Catalogue starter data — review before launch

This package includes 24 public Nilkamal model references to make the shop easier to configure. They are **not verified Navnidh Traders inventory**. Every seeded item starts with stock `0` so the website does not accidentally accept an order for an item you do not have.

- Public reference prices can change and may not match your purchase price or selling price. Edit them in the Admin dashboard.
- 10 models use product images hosted by Nilkamal Homes. These are external image URLs, not bundled files. Confirm you have permission to use them on a commercial site; replace with your own product photography or authorized supplier assets if not.
- Other items use original illustrative SVG artwork labelled “Illustrative Catalogue Image”; it is not a photograph of that exact model. Replace it with your own/authorized product photo before you market that model as available.
- To make an item purchasable, verify its exact variant and selling price in Admin and set its real stock quantity. Do not set stock to a guessed value.
- See `CATALOG_STARTER.csv` for the full list and fields to verify.

Official reference collection: https://www.nilkamalhomes.com/collections/office-chairs and https://www.nilkamalfurniture.com/collections/office-chairs
